Responsible AI & the LawZimmedaar AI aur Kanoon

A loan-advice bot that quietly gives worse answers in Tamil than in English. A hiring assistant that learned to prefer one college. A support bot that stores customers' Aadhaar numbers in its logs forever. None of these teams meant harm. Responsible AI is the engineering discipline of not causing it by accident, and the law increasingly requires it.

Loan-salaah bot jo chupchaap Tamil mein English se kharab jawab deta hai. Hiring assistant jisne ek college ko pasand karna seekh liya. Support bot jo customers ke Aadhaar numbers hamesha ke liye logs mein rakhta hai. In mein se kisi team ka iraada nuksaan ka nahi tha. Responsible AI galti se nuksaan na karne ki engineering hai, aur kanoon ise badhte roop se zaroori bana raha hai.

AI behaviour

Models carry society's biasesModels samaaj ke bias saath laate hain

Models learn from human writing, including its stereotypes about gender, caste, religion, region and language. They may also be measurably worse in some languages. You can't remove bias with one prompt; you can measure it (same question, swap only the name, city or language, compare answers), reduce it, keep humans on high-stakes decisions, and document what you found.

Models insaani lekhan se seekhte hain, ling, jaati, dharm, kshetra aur bhasha ke baare mein uske stereotypes samet. Kuch bhashaon mein woh naapne laayak kharab bhi ho sakte hain. Ek prompt se bias nahi hatta; aap use naap sakte ho (same sawaal, sirf naam, shehar ya bhasha badlo, jawab compare karo), kam kar sakte ho, bade daav ke faislon par insaan rakh sakte ho, aur jo mila use document kar sakte ho.

Engineer's note

Privacy by defaultDefault mein privacy

Collect the minimum. Mask personal data before it reaches a model (Book 1's redactor handles Aadhaar, PAN, phone, card). Log hashes instead of raw prompts unless you truly need them. Set retention periods. Check each provider's data terms (training use, retention, region). These defaults make most compliance questions easy.

Kam se kam jama karo. Model tak pahunchne se pehle personal data chhupao (Book 1 ka redactor Aadhaar, PAN, phone, card sambhaalta hai). Jab tak sach mein zaroorat na ho, raw prompts ki jagah hash log karo. Retention periods tay karo. Har provider ki data terms check karo (training use, retention, region). Yeh defaults zyaadatar compliance sawaal aasaan bana dete hain.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Design against bias, privacy harm, hidden AI, copyright and unsafe actions.
  2. India's DPDP Act, sector rules, and (for EU users) GDPR and the EU AI Act shape what you build.
  3. Measure bias by swapping names, places and languages.
  4. Privacy by default; humans on high-stakes decisions; document everything.
  1. Bias, privacy nuksaan, chhupa AI, copyright aur asurakshit kaamon ke khilaaf design karo.
  2. India ka DPDP Act, sector niyam, aur (EU users ke liye) GDPR aur EU AI Act tay karte hain aap kya banaate ho.
  3. Naam, jagah aur bhasha badalkar bias naapo.
  4. Default mein privacy; bade daav ke faislon par insaan; sab document karo.
