Synthetic DataSynthetic Data

You need 500 test questions in Hinglish for your support bot, but launch is next week and you have 12 real ones. Or you need training examples of rare cases (a fraud attempt, an angry customer) that almost never happen. Models can write them: this is synthetic data. Done well, it fills gaps quickly. Done badly, it teaches your system a fake world.

Aapko support bot ke liye Hinglish mein 500 test sawaal chahiye, par launch agle hafte hai aur aapke paas 12 asli hain. Ya aapko aise rare cases ke training examples chahiye (fraud ki koshish, gussa customer) jo lagbhag kabhi nahi hote. Models unhe likh sakte hain: ise synthetic data kehte hain. Achhe se kiya toh khaali jagah jaldi bharta hai. Bure se kiya toh aapke system ko nakli duniya sikhaata hai.

Variety on purposeJaan-boojh kar variety

Ask a model for "50 customer complaints" and you'll get 50 polite, grammatical, English, medium-length complaints that sound like the same person. Real customers aren't like that. Feed the generator explicit dimensions (persona, language, mood, length, typos, topic) and combine them, so every example is a different point in the space.

Model se "50 customer shikaayatein" maango toh 50 vinamra, sahi grammar wali, English, madhyam lambi shikaayatein milengi jo ek hi insaan jaisi lagti hain. Asli customers aise nahi hote. Generator ko saaf dimensions do (persona, bhasha, mood, lambaai, typos, vishay) aur unhe milao, taaki har example space mein alag bindu ho.

3

Uses and dangersUse aur khatre

AI behaviour

Models write in their own voiceModels apni awaaz mein likhte hain

Synthetic text carries the generator's habits: favourite words, tidy structure, a polite tone, its blind spots and biases. Train or test only on that, and your system learns to handle the model's imitation of customers, not customers. Train models repeatedly on model output and quality degrades over generations (model collapse). Always mix in and validate against real data.

Synthetic text generator ki aadatein laata hai: pasandeeda shabd, saaf structure, vinamra tone, uske andhe kone aur bias. Sirf us par train ya test karo toh aapka system customers ki model wali nakal sambhaalna seekhta hai, customers nahi. Models ko baar-baar model output par train karo toh peedhiyon mein quality girti hai (model collapse). Hamesha asli data milaao aur usse validate karo.

Engineer's note

Synthetic tests are the best first useSynthetic tests sabse achha pehla use

The safest, highest-value use is building eval sets before you have real traffic: edge cases, adversarial inputs, other languages, every product category. Have a human review each one (it's faster to fix 200 drafted cases than to write 200 from scratch), then replace them gradually with real failures from production.

Sabse surakshit, sabse keemti use hai asli traffic se pehle eval sets banana: edge cases, hamle wale inputs, doosri bhashayein, har product category. Har ek insaan se review karwao (200 draft cases theek karna 200 shuru se likhne se tez hai), phir dheere-dheere production ke asli failures se badlo.

In the real world

Synthetic data for privacyPrivacy ke liye synthetic data

Teams use synthetic records shaped like real ones (same fields, realistic values, fake people) so developers and vendors can work without touching customer data. Generated names, Aadhaar-like numbers and addresses must be clearly fake, and the generator must not have memorised real records. Check terms of service too: some providers restrict using outputs to train competing models.

Teams asli jaise synthetic records use karti hain (same fields, asli jaisi values, nakli log) taaki developers aur vendors customer data chhue bina kaam kar sakein. Banaaye naam, Aadhaar jaise numbers aur pate saaf nakli hone chahiye, aur generator ne asli records yaad na kiye hon. Terms of service bhi check karo: kuch providers competing models train karne ke liye outputs use karna rokte hain.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Synthetic data = seeds → generate with explicit variety → filter → dedupe → human check.
  2. Its best first use is test sets for cases you don't have yet.
  3. It carries the generator's voice and biases; always validate against real data.
  4. Use it to protect privacy, with clearly fake identities.
  1. Synthetic data = seeds → saaf variety ke saath banao → filter → dedupe → insaani check.
  2. Iska sabse achha pehla use un cases ke test sets hain jo abhi aapke paas nahi.
  3. Isme generator ki awaaz aur bias hota hai; hamesha asli data se validate karo.
  4. Privacy bachaane ke liye use karo, saaf nakli pehchaanon ke saath.
