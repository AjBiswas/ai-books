Reasoning ModelsSochne Wale Models

Some questions need a quick reply; some need real thought. A reasoning model is a model trained to think privately before it answers: to try an approach, notice a mistake, and try again. That thinking is extra tokens, so it costs time and money. Knowing when it's worth paying for is a core AI-engineering judgement.

Kuch sawaalon ko jaldi jawab chahiye; kuch ko asli soch. Reasoning model aisa model hai jo jawab se pehle chupchaap sochne ke liye train hua hai: ek tareeka try karna, galti pakadna, aur phir try karna. Woh sochna extra tokens hai, isliye samay aur paisa lagta hai. Kab iska paisa dena sahi hai, yeh AI engineering ka ek mukhya faisla hai.

Blurting vs thinkingJaldi bol dena vs sochna

RememberYaad rakho

Reasoning models learned to think through reinforcement learning on checkable problems (maths with known answers, code with tests): long thoughts that reached right answers were rewarded. So they shine on problems with a right answer you can check, and help less on taste, tone or creativity.

Reasoning models ne sochna check hone laayak sawaalon par reinforcement learning se seekha (jaane hue jawab wale maths, tests wala code): sahi jawab tak pahunchne wali lambi soch ko inaam mila. Isliye woh sahi-jawab-wale aur check hone laayak sawaalon par chamakte hain, aur pasand, tone ya creativity mein kam madad karte hain.

2

The effort dialEffort ka dial

Most providers now give you a dial instead of an on/off switch: how hard should the model think? On current Claude models it's called effort (low, medium, high, xhigh, max); OpenAI calls it reasoning effort. More effort means more thinking tokens, longer waits and higher cost, with better results only on genuinely hard problems. The bars below show the typical shape; measure your own numbers.

Zyaadatar providers ab on/off switch ki jagah dial dete hain: model kitna zor se soche? Current Claude models par ise effort kehte hain (low, medium, high, xhigh, max); OpenAI ise reasoning effort kehta hai. Zyaada effort matlab zyaada thinking tokens, lamba intezaar aur zyaada kharch, aur behtar nateeje sirf sach mein mushkil sawaalon par. Neeche ke bars aam shape dikhaate hain; apne numbers khud naapo.

3

When is thinking worth it?Sochna kab faayde ka hai?

AI behaviour

Overthinking is realZaroorat se zyaada sochna sach hai

On easy questions, reasoning models can spend hundreds of tokens second-guessing a one-line answer. On our laptop in Chapter 4, a one-sentence definition took 329–2,149 tokens. In agents, high effort can also mean more tool calls and longer runs. Match effort to the task: low for chat and classification, higher for multi-step coding and planning.

Aasaan sawaalon par reasoning models ek-line ke jawab par saikadon tokens shak karne mein kharch kar sakte hain. Chapter 4 mein hamare laptop par ek sentence ki definition ne 329–2,149 tokens liye. Agents mein high effort ka matlab zyaada tool calls aur lambe runs bhi ho sakta hai. Effort kaam ke hisaab se rakho: chat aur classification ke liye low, multi-step coding aur planning ke liye zyaada.

Engineer's note

Prompting reasoning models is differentReasoning models ko prompt karna alag hai

Don't write "think step by step"; they already do. Give the full goal, constraints and what "done" looks like up front, then let them work. Thinking tokens are billed as output tokens, and many APIs return only a summary of the thinking, or none. If you show progress to users, request the summarised display option or stream progress updates.

"Step by step socho" mat likho; woh pehle se sochte hain. Poora lakshya, seemaayein aur "done" kaisa dikhta hai, shuru mein hi do, phir unhe kaam karne do. Thinking tokens output tokens ki tarah bill hote hain, aur kai APIs soch ka sirf summary lautaati hain, ya kuch nahi. Users ko progress dikhaani ho toh summarised display option maango ya progress updates stream karo.

In the real world

"Test-time compute" is the 2025–26 headline"Test-time compute" 2025–26 ki surkhi hai

For years, models got better mainly by training bigger. Reasoning models opened a second dial: spend more compute at answer time. That's why one model can be cheap for chat and brilliant for a hard proof, and why cost forecasting must include thinking tokens. Teams often route: low effort by default, high effort only when a classifier or a failed first attempt says the task is hard.

Saalon tak models zyaadatar bade train hokar behtar hue. Reasoning models ne doosra dial khola: jawab ke waqt zyaada compute lagao. Isliye ek hi model chat ke liye sasta aur mushkil proof ke liye shaandaar ho sakta hai, aur kharch ke andaaze mein thinking tokens shaamil hone chahiye. Teams aksar route karti hain: default low effort, high effort tabhi jab classifier ya fail hui pehli koshish kahe kaam mushkil hai.

For coders: setting effortCoders ke liye: effort set karnapython 
resp = client.messages.create(
    model="claude-opus-5-5",
    max_tokens=16000,
    thinking={"type": "adaptive", "display": "summarized"},   # show a readable summary of the reasoning
    output_config={"effort": "high"},                          # low | medium | high | xhigh | max
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Plan a 3-step migration of our orders table with zero downtime."}],
)
for block in resp.content:
    if block.type == "thinking": print("THINKING:", block.thinking)
    elif block.type == "text":   print("ANSWER:", block.text)

On Claude Opus 5.5 thinking is always on and the default effort is medium; lower effort is how you make it faster and cheaper. Laptop models via Ollama use a think flag, with the caveats measured in Chapter 4.

Claude Opus 5.5 par thinking hamesha on hai aur default effort medium hai; kam effort se ise tez aur sasta banaate ho. Ollama ke laptop models think flag use karte hain, Chapter 4 mein naapi gayi saavdhaaniyon ke saath.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Reasoning models think before answering; thinking is billed output.
  2. They help most on checkable, multi-step problems.
  3. Use the effort dial: low by default, high when the task is truly hard.
  4. Give them the whole goal up front; don't tell them how to think.
  1. Reasoning models jawab se pehle sochte hain; sochna output ki tarah bill hota hai.
  2. Check hone laayak, multi-step sawaalon par sabse zyaada madad.
  3. Effort dial use karo: default low, kaam sach mein mushkil ho toh high.
  4. Poora lakshya shuru mein do; unhe sochna mat sikhaao.
