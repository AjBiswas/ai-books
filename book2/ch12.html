Multimodal AIMultimodal AI

Haron used to be text-only. Now it can look at a photo of a broken parcel, read a scanned invoice, listen to a customer on the phone, talk back in a natural voice, and even draw. Models that handle more than text are called multimodal. The trick to understanding them: every kind of input is turned into tokens, so everything you learned about tokens, context and cost still applies.

Haron pehle sirf text samajhta tha. Ab woh toote parcel ki photo dekh sakta hai, scanned invoice padh sakta hai, phone par customer ko sun sakta hai, natural awaaz mein jawab de sakta hai, aur draw bhi kar sakta hai. Text se zyaada sambhaalne wale models multimodal kehlaate hain. Unhe samajhne ki chaabi: har tarah ka input tokens mein badalta hai, isliye tokens, context aur kharch ke baare mein seekha sab kuch ab bhi laagu hai.

1

A picture is worth ~1,600 tokensEk tasveer ≈ 1,600 tokens

A vision model cuts an image into small square patches, turns each patch into a token-like vector, and reads them alongside your text. On Claude, an image costs roughly (width × height) ÷ 750 tokens, so a 1,092 × 1,092 photo is about 1,600 tokens. Bigger images are resized down. Every image is input you pay for.

Vision model image ko chhote chaukor patches mein kaatta hai, har patch ko token jaisa vector banata hai, aur aapke text ke saath padhta hai. Claude par ek image lagbhag (width × height) ÷ 750 tokens leti hai, toh 1,092 × 1,092 photo lagbhag 1,600 tokens. Badi images chhoti ki jaati hain. Har image input hai jiska paisa lagta hai.

AI behaviour

Vision models misread small detailsVision models chhoti details galat padhte hain

They're excellent at "what's in this picture?" and good at reading clear documents, but they can misread small digits, count objects wrongly, and confuse left and right. For invoices and IDs, extract into a schema (Chapter 8), then validate numbers with code (does the total equal the sum of the lines? is the GSTIN checksum valid?).

"Is tasveer mein kya hai?" mein shaandaar aur saaf documents padhne mein achhe, par chhote digits galat padh sakte hain, cheezein galat gin sakte hain, aur left-right mein confuse ho sakte hain. Invoices aur IDs ke liye schema mein nikaalo (Chapter 8), phir numbers code se validate karo (kya total lines ke jod ke barabar hai? kya GSTIN checksum sahi hai?).

2

Talking to Haron: two designsHaron se baat: do design

A voice agent can be a pipeline of three models (speech-to-text → LLM → text-to-speech), or one speech-to-speech model that hears and speaks directly. The pipeline is easier to control and debug; speech-to-speech feels more natural and responds faster. Either way, a human expects a reply within about a second.

Voice agent teen models ki pipeline ho sakta hai (speech-to-text → LLM → text-to-speech), ya ek speech-to-speech model jo seedhe sunta aur bolta hai. Pipeline control aur debug karna aasaan; speech-to-speech zyaada natural lagta aur jaldi jawab deta hai. Dono mein insaan lagbhag ek second mein jawab ki ummeed karta hai.

What multimodal is good forMultimodal kis kaam ka hai

Engineer's note

Documents: try the model before building an OCR pipelineDocuments: OCR pipeline banaane se pehle model try karo

Many APIs accept PDFs directly and read both the text and the page images, including tables and charts. For clean documents this often beats a traditional OCR pipeline with far less code. For thousands of pages a day, compare cost: dedicated OCR plus a small text model can be much cheaper than sending page images to a large model.

Kai APIs seedhe PDFs leti hain aur text aur page images dono padhti hain, tables aur charts samet. Saaf documents ke liye yeh aksar purani OCR pipeline ko kahin kam code mein hara deta hai. Roz hazaaron pages ke liye kharch compare karo: dedicated OCR aur chhota text model, bade model ko page images bhejne se kahin sasta ho sakta hai.

In the real world

Voice in IndiaIndia mein voice

For many Indian users, speaking is easier than typing, especially in their own language. Voice agents for banks, telecom and government services are a fast-growing area. Test speech recognition on real accents, code-mixed Hinglish and noisy phone lines; Indic-focused speech models (for example from AI4Bharat and Sarvam) often beat global ones here. Always disclose that callers are talking to an AI.

Kai Indian users ke liye bolna type karne se aasaan hai, khaaskar apni bhasha mein. Banks, telecom aur sarkari sevaon ke voice agents tezi se badhta kshetra hai. Speech recognition ko asli lehjon, code-mixed Hinglish aur shor wali phone lines par test karo; Indic-focused speech models (jaise AI4Bharat aur Sarvam ke) yahan aksar global models ko harate hain. Hamesha batao ki caller AI se baat kar raha hai.

For coders: sending an imageCoders ke liye: image bhejnapython 
import base64, anthropic
img = base64.standard_b64encode(open("parcel.jpg", "rb").read()).decode()
client = anthropic.Anthropic()
resp = client.messages.create(model="claude-opus-5-5", max_tokens=1024, messages=[{
    "role": "user",
    "content": [
        {"type": "image", "source": {"type": "base64", "media_type": "image/jpeg", "data": img}},
        {"type": "text", "text": "Is this parcel damaged? Reply DAMAGED or OK, then one sentence of evidence."},
    ]}])

Put the image before the question. Locally, Ollama runs vision models too (e.g. ollama pull qwen2.5vl or gemma3), with images passed in the message's images field.

Image sawaal se pehle rakho. Local mein Ollama vision models bhi chalaata hai (jaise ollama pull qwen2.5vl ya gemma3), images message ke images field mein.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Images, PDFs and audio all become tokens; they cost context and money.
  2. Check small details from images with code.
  3. Voice agents: pipeline for control, speech-to-speech for natural speed.
  4. Test on real accents and languages, and always disclose AI.
  1. Images, PDFs aur audio sab tokens bante hain; context aur paisa lete hain.
  2. Images ki chhoti details code se check karo.
  3. Voice agents: control ke liye pipeline, natural speed ke liye speech-to-speech.
  4. Asli lehjon aur bhashaon par test karo, aur hamesha batao ki AI hai.
