Embeddings & Vector DatabasesEmbeddings aur Vector Databases

A customer types "I forgot my login credentials". Your help centre has an article titled "How do I reset my password?". Not a single word matches, yet a human sees instantly they're the same thing. Embeddings let a computer see it too: they turn any text into a list of numbers, a point on a meaning map, where similar meanings sit close together.

Customer likhta hai "I forgot my login credentials". Aapke help centre mein article hai "How do I reset my password?". Ek bhi shabd match nahi karta, phir bhi insaan turant samajh jaata hai ki dono ek hi baat hain. Embeddings computer ko bhi yeh dikhaati hain: woh kisi bhi text ko numbers ki list banaati hain, meaning map par ek bindu, jahan milte-julte matlab paas-paas baithte hain.

1

The meaning mapMatlab ka naksha

An embedding model reads a sentence and outputs a fixed-length list of numbers (384 for the model below, often 768–3,072). Think of each list as GPS coordinates on a map with hundreds of directions instead of two. To compare two texts, measure the angle between their arrows: cosine similarity, from about −1 (opposite) through 0 (unrelated) to 1 (same meaning).

Embedding model ek sentence padhkar tay lambaai ki numbers ki list deta hai (neeche ke model mein 384, aksar 768–3,072). Har list ko aise naksha ke GPS coordinates socho jisme do nahi, saikadon dishayein hain. Do texts compare karne ke liye unke teeron ke beech ka kon naapo: cosine similarity, lagbhag −1 (ulta) se 0 (bematlab) se 1 (same matlab) tak.

2

Measured on a laptopLaptop par naapa

We embedded five sentences with all-minilm (a 46 MB model running in Ollama) and compared each to "How do I reset my password?". The English paraphrase scores high. The Hinglish one, with the same meaning, scores much lower, because this small model was trained mostly on English. That one result is a whole lesson.

Humne paanch sentences ko all-minilm (Ollama mein chalne wala 46 MB ka model) se embed kiya aur har ek ko "How do I reset my password?" se compare kiya. English paraphrase ka score zyaada hai. Same matlab wala Hinglish sentence kahin kam score karta hai, kyunki yeh chhota model zyaadatar English par train hua tha. Yeh ek nateeja poora sabak hai.

RememberYaad rakho

Pick an embedding model for your languages and your domain. For Indian languages and Hinglish, use a multilingual model (for example bge-m3, multilingual-e5, or a provider's multilingual embedding API) and test it on your real queries. Embeddings from different models are not compatible: if you switch models, re-embed everything.

Embedding model apni bhashaon aur apne domain ke liye chuno. Indian bhashaon aur Hinglish ke liye multilingual model lo (jaise bge-m3, multilingual-e5, ya provider ki multilingual embedding API) aur apni asli queries par test karo. Alag models ki embeddings compatible nahi hoti: model badlo toh sab dobara embed karo.

3

Searching a million points fastDas laakh bindu tezi se search karna

Comparing a query against every stored vector works for thousands of items. For millions, a vector database builds an index (often HNSW, a "small-world" graph) that finds the nearest neighbours in milliseconds by hopping between close points, trading a tiny bit of accuracy for huge speed.

Query ko har stored vector se compare karna hazaaron items tak chalta hai. Laakhon ke liye vector database ek index banata hai (aksar HNSW, ek "small-world" graph) jo paas ke bindu par kood-kood kar milliseconds mein sabse kareeb padosi dhoondhta hai, thodi si accuracy ke badle bahut speed.

AI behaviour

Embeddings miss exact thingsEmbeddings sateek cheezein miss karti hain

Embeddings are great at meaning, weak at exact strings: an order ID, an error code, a product SKU, a person's name. "ERR-4031" and "ERR-4013" look almost identical to an embedding model. That's why real systems combine vector search with keyword search (hybrid search, Chapter 14).

Embeddings matlab mein shaandaar, sateek strings mein kamzor: order ID, error code, product SKU, kisi ka naam. Embedding model ko "ERR-4031" aur "ERR-4013" lagbhag ek jaise dikhte hain. Isliye asli systems vector search ko keyword search ke saath milaate hain (hybrid search, Chapter 14).

✓

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. An embedding is a point on a meaning map; cosine similarity measures closeness.
  2. Choose an embedding model that knows your languages, and test it.
  3. Vector databases use indexes like HNSW to search millions of points fast.
  4. Embeddings miss exact IDs and codes, so pair them with keyword search.
  1. Embedding matlab ke naksha par ek bindu hai; cosine similarity nazdeeki naapti hai.
  2. Aisa embedding model chuno jo aapki bhashayein jaane, aur test karo.
  3. Vector databases HNSW jaise indexes se laakhon bindu tezi se search karte hain.
  4. Embeddings sateek IDs aur codes miss karti hain, isliye keyword search ke saath jodo.
