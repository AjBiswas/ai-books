Fine-TuningFine-Tuning

Fine-tuning means continuing a model's training on your own examples, so its behaviour changes permanently. It sounds like the obvious way to "teach Haron our business". Usually it isn't: prompting, RAG and tools (Chapters 6–19) solve most needs faster and cheaper. But for a few jobs, such as a consistent house style, a narrow task at huge volume, or making a small model act like a big one, fine-tuning is exactly right. This chapter shows when, and how, on hardware you might actually have.

Fine-tuning ka matlab hai model ki training ko aapke examples par aage badhaana, taaki uska behaviour hamesha ke liye badle. "Haron ko hamara business sikhaana" ka yeh seedha tareeka lagta hai. Aksar nahi hota: prompting, RAG aur tools (Chapter 6–19) zyaadatar zaroorat jaldi aur saste mein poori karte hain. Par kuch kaamon ke liye, jaise ek jaisa house style, bade volume par sankara kaam, ya chhote model ko bade jaisa banana, fine-tuning bilkul sahi hai. Yeh chapter dikhata hai kab, aur kaise, us hardware par jo shayad aapke paas ho.

For and not forKiske liye aur kiske liye nahi

RememberYaad rakho

Fine-tuning changes how a model behaves far more reliably than what it knows. To add facts that change (prices, policies, stock), use RAG. A model fine-tuned on last month's price list will confidently quote last month's prices forever.

Fine-tuning model ke kaise behave karne ko badalta hai, kya jaanta hai use kahin kam bharose se. Badalne wale facts (daam, policies, stock) jodne ke liye RAG use karo. Pichhle mahine ki price list par fine-tune hua model hamesha yakeen se pichhle mahine ke daam batayega.

2

LoRA: train a tiny add-on, not the whole modelLoRA: poora model nahi, chhota add-on train karo

Full fine-tuning updates billions of numbers and needs huge GPUs. LoRA freezes the model and trains two thin matrices beside each big one; their product is the change. It works because useful changes tend to be low-rank: they can be described with few directions. Our lab fakes such a change and measures how much a rank-r LoRA captures.

Poora fine-tuning arbon numbers badalta hai aur bade GPUs chahiye. LoRA model ko jama deta hai aur har badi matrix ke saath do patli matrices train karta hai; unka guna hi badlaav hai. Yeh isliye chalta hai kyunki kaam ke badlaav aksar low-rank hote hain: kam dishaon se bayaan ho jaate hain. Hamara lab aisa badlaav banata hai aur naapta hai ki rank-r LoRA kitna pakadta hai.

The workflowWorkflow

AI behaviour

Fine-tuning can break things you didn't touchFine-tuning un cheezon ko tod sakta hai jinhe chhua bhi nahi

Train hard on one task and the model can get worse at others (catastrophic forgetting), copy mistakes in your data, or lose some of its safety behaviour; researchers have shown even small fine-tunes can weaken refusals. Keep a general eval and a safety eval alongside your task eval, and compare before and after.

Ek kaam par zor se train karo toh model doosron mein kharab ho sakta hai (catastrophic forgetting), aapke data ki galtiyaan copy kar sakta hai, ya apna kuch safety behaviour kho sakta hai; researchers ne dikhaaya hai ki chhote fine-tunes bhi refusals kamzor kar sakte hain. Task eval ke saath general eval aur safety eval rakho, aur pehle-baad compare karo.

Engineer's note

Data quality beats data quantityData ki quality maatra se upar

A few hundred excellent, varied, correctly formatted examples often beat ten thousand noisy ones. Read a random sample of your training data yourself before training. Use the exact chat template the model expects, hold out 10–20% for evaluation, and start with a small LoRA rank (8–16) and a few epochs.

Kuch sau behtareen, alag-alag, sahi format wale examples aksar das hazaar shor wale examples ko harate hain. Training se pehle apne training data ka random sample khud padho. Model ka sateek chat template use karo, 10–20% evaluation ke liye alag rakho, aur chhote LoRA rank (8–16) aur kuch epochs se shuru karo.

In the real world

Where fine-tuning happensFine-tuning kahan hota hai

Two routes: provider fine-tuning APIs (upload examples, get a private model endpoint; easy, but the model stays on their platform) and open-weights fine-tuning with libraries like Hugging Face TRL, Unsloth or Axolotl on your own or rented GPUs (full control; you serve it yourself). Check the model licence and the provider's terms before training on outputs from another model.

Do raaste: provider fine-tuning APIs (examples upload karo, private model endpoint pao; aasaan, par model unke platform par rehta hai) aur open-weights fine-tuning, Hugging Face TRL, Unsloth ya Axolotl jaisi libraries se apne ya kiraaye ke GPUs par (poora control; serve aap karte ho). Doosre model ke outputs par train karne se pehle model licence aur provider ki terms check karo.

Deep dive: the maths of LoRAGehraai mein: LoRA ka mathsadvanced
W′ = W + ΔW, ΔW = B·A, B ∈ ℝd×r, A ∈ ℝr×d, r ≪ d

Instead of learning a full d×d update (16.8 M numbers for d = 4,096), LoRA learns B and A with only 2·d·r numbers (65,536 for r = 8). W stays frozen. The adapter can be merged into W for serving, or kept separate so one base model serves many adapters. QLoRA stores the frozen W in 4-bit and trains the adapters in higher precision, which is how a 7–8B model can be fine-tuned on a single consumer GPU. SFT trains on input→ideal-output pairs; DPO trains on chosen-vs-rejected pairs (Book 1, Chapter 4).

Poora d×d update (d = 4,096 ke liye 1.68 crore numbers) seekhne ki jagah LoRA sirf 2·d·r numbers ke saath B aur A seekhta hai (r = 8 ke liye 65,536). W jama rehta hai. Adapter serving ke liye W mein mila sakte ho, ya alag rakh sakte ho taaki ek base model kai adapters serve kare. QLoRA jame W ko 4-bit mein rakhta hai aur adapters zyaada precision mein train karta hai, isi se 7–8B model ek consumer GPU par fine-tune hota hai. SFT input→behtareen-output pairs par train karta hai; DPO chuna-vs-thukraaya pairs par (Book 1, Chapter 4).

✓

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Fine-tune for behaviour, style and narrow high-volume tasks, not for changing facts.
  2. LoRA trains a tiny add-on; QLoRA makes it fit on one consumer GPU.
  3. Quality data, the right template, a held-out eval, and a safety check.
  4. Always compare against a well-prompted base model first.
  1. Fine-tune behaviour, style aur sankare bade-volume kaamon ke liye karo, badalne wale facts ke liye nahi.
  2. LoRA chhota add-on train karta hai; QLoRA use ek consumer GPU par fit karta hai.
  3. Quality data, sahi template, alag rakha eval, aur safety check.
  4. Hamesha pehle achhe se prompt kiye base model se compare karo.
