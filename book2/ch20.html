Multi-Agent Systems & A2AMulti-Agent Systems aur A2A

Some jobs are too big or too varied for one Haron. A research task across 20 sources, a company where the HR agent needs to ask the IT agent something, a team of specialists reviewing a contract from different angles. Multi-agent systems split work across several agents. And when those agents belong to different teams or companies, they need a common language to talk: that's what the A2A (Agent2Agent) protocol provides.

Kuch kaam ek Haron ke liye bahut bade ya bahut alag hain. 20 sources mein research, ek company jahan HR agent ko IT agent se kuch poochna ho, specialists ki team jo contract alag nazariye se review kare. Multi-agent systems kaam kai agents mein baant-te hain. Aur jab woh agents alag teams ya companies ke hon, unhe baat karne ke liye common bhasha chahiye: yahi A2A (Agent2Agent) protocol deta hai.

MCP connects tools; A2A connects agentsMCP tools jodta hai; A2A agents jodta hai

MCP (Chapter 18) lets an agent use a tool: something passive that does one thing when called. A2A lets an agent delegate to another agent: something that has its own model, plans its own steps, may take hours, and reports back. Each A2A agent publishes an Agent Card (a JSON file describing what it can do and how to reach it), and work is exchanged as tasks with states like submitted, working, input-required and completed.

MCP (Chapter 18) agent ko tool use karne deta hai: kuch passive jo call hone par ek kaam karta hai. A2A agent ko doosre agent ko kaam saunpne deta hai: jiska apna model ho, jo apne steps khud plan kare, ghanton le sakta hai, aur report wapas kare. Har A2A agent ek Agent Card publish karta hai (JSON file jo batati hai woh kya kar sakta hai aur kaise pahunchein), aur kaam tasks ki tarah badla jaata hai jinki states hoti hain jaise submitted, working, input-required aur completed.

AI behaviour

The telephone gameChinese whispers

Every hand-off between agents is a summary, and every summary can drop a detail. "Kannada voice assistant, Android only, ₹99/month" becomes "launched a voice assistant" becomes "active in AI". Ask agents to pass structured facts with sources, not prose, and give the final agent access to the original evidence when accuracy matters.

Agents ke beech har handoff ek summary hai, aur har summary detail gira sakti hai. "Kannada voice assistant, sirf Android, ₹99/mahina" ban jaata hai "voice assistant launch kiya" ban jaata hai "AI mein active". Agents se kahani nahi, source ke saath structured facts aage badhwao, aur jab accuracy zaroori ho toh aakhri agent ko asli saboot tak pahunch do.

Engineer's note

When a team beats one agentKab team ek agent ko harati hai

Use several agents when the work splits into independent parts (research N companies), when the total reading won't fit one context, or when specialists need different tools or permissions. Stay with one agent when steps depend tightly on each other, such as writing one coherent program, where coordination overhead and lost context hurt more than parallelism helps.

Kai agents tab use karo jab kaam alag hisson mein bate (N companies ki research), jab kul padhaai ek context mein na aaye, ya specialists ko alag tools ya permissions chahiye. Ek agent par raho jab steps ek-doosre par kas ke depend hon, jaise ek juda hua program likhna, jahan taal-mel ka bojh aur khoya context parallelism ki madad se zyaada nuksaan karta hai.

In the real world

Agents across company wallsCompany ki deewaron ke paar agents

A2A was launched by Google in 2025 with many partners and moved to the Linux Foundation as an open project. The promise: your procurement agent negotiates with a supplier's sales agent without either company exposing its internals. The open questions are the business ones: who is liable for what an agent agreed to, how agents prove identity, and how to audit a conversation between two companies' AIs.

A2A Google ne 2025 mein kai partners ke saath launch kiya aur yeh open project ki tarah Linux Foundation mein gaya. Vaada: aapka procurement agent supplier ke sales agent se mol-bhaav kare, bina kisi company ke andar ki baatein khole. Khule sawaal business ke hain: agent ne jo maana uski zimmedaari kiski, agents pehchaan kaise saabit karein, aur do companies ke AIs ki baatcheet ka audit kaise ho.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Teams come in shapes: orchestrator–workers, handoffs, and debate or review.
  2. MCP plugs in tools; A2A lets agents delegate to other agents.
  3. Teams cost many times more tokens and can lose details.
  4. Use a team only when the work truly splits.
  1. Teams ki shapes: orchestrator–workers, handoffs, aur debate ya review.
  2. MCP tools lagaata hai; A2A agents ko doosre agents ko kaam saunpne deta hai.
  3. Teams kai guna zyaada tokens leti hain aur details kho sakti hain.
  4. Team tabhi jab kaam sach mein bate.
