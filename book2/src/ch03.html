Walk into the model zoo and there are hundreds of animals: giant ones that live only in the provider's cloud, medium ones anyone can download, tiny ones that fit in a phone. Some cost money per word, some are "free" in very different senses of the word. Haron gives you the tour, and a simple way to choose.

Kuch har word ka paisa lete hain, kuch "free" hain par "free" ke bilkul alag matlab mein. Haron aapko ghumaata hai, aur chunne ka seedha tareeka batata hai.

RememberYaad rakho

Open-weights means you can download the model's numbers and run it yourself. It doesn't always mean "open source" (training data and code are usually not released), and the licence may limit commercial use. Always read the licence.

Open-weights ka matlab hai aap model ke numbers download karke khud chala sakte ho. Iska matlab hamesha "open source" nahi (training data aur code aksar release nahi hote), aur licence commercial use par rok laga sakta hai. Licence hamesha padho.

Five questions to pick a modelModel chunne ke paanch sawaal

AI behaviour

Leaderboards don't know your taskLeaderboards aapka kaam nahi jaante

Public rankings (benchmarks and arenas) are a useful first filter, but a model that tops a maths benchmark may be worse at your Hinglish support tickets than a cheaper one. Models are also tuned to do well on popular benchmarks. The only ranking that counts is your own eval on 50–200 of your real examples (Chapter 26).

Public rankings (benchmarks aur arenas) pehla filter ke roop mein kaam ke hain, par maths benchmark mein top model aapke Hinglish support tickets par ek saste model se bhi kharab ho sakta hai. Models popular benchmarks par achha karne ke liye tune bhi kiye jaate hain. Sirf ek ranking maayne rakhti hai: aapke 50–200 asli examples par aapka apna eval (Chapter 26).

Engineer's note

Start big, then go smallBade se shuru karo, phir chhote par jao

Prototype with a top model so you learn what's possible. Build your eval set. Then try cheaper tiers and open models against that eval, and move each task to the cheapest model that still passes. Many teams end up using two or three models: a small one for easy, high-volume work and a big one for hard cases (routing, Chapter 29).

Prototype top model se banao taaki pata chale kya mumkin hai. Apna eval set banao. Phir us eval par saste tiers aur open models try karo, aur har kaam ko sabse saste pass hone wale model par le jao. Kai teams aakhir mein do-teen models use karti hain: aasaan, zyaada volume ke kaam ke liye chhota aur mushkil cases ke liye bada (routing, Chapter 29).

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Closed models are strongest and easiest; open-weights give control; small models run anywhere.
  2. "Free" always costs something: limits, data, hardware or your time.
  3. Price tiers differ by 40× or more; pick per task, not per company.
  4. Choose with five questions, then confirm with your own eval.
  1. Closed models sabse majboot aur aasaan; open-weights control dete hain; chhote models kahin bhi chalte hain.
  2. "Free" ki bhi keemat hai: limits, data, hardware ya aapka samay.
  3. Price tiers mein 40× ya zyaada farak; company nahi, har kaam ke hisaab se chuno.
  4. Paanch sawaalon se chuno, phir apne eval se pakka karo.
