Models and trainingModels aur training

Some people also use it for multimodal models; check the context.Aksar Masked Language Model (jaise BERT): chhupe shabd bhar kar seekhta hai, text samajhne mein badhiya. VLMVision-Language Model: reads images and text together, e.g. answers questions about a photo or a scanned invoice.Vision-Language Model: image aur text saath padhta hai, jaise photo ya scan invoice par sawaal ka jawab.Ch 12

Context, memory and retrievalContext, memory aur retrieval

Context engineeringDeciding exactly what goes into each model call: instructions, facts, history, tools, and in what order.Har model call mein kya jaayega, yeh tay karna: instructions, facts, history, tools, aur kis kram mein.Ch 10
Prompt engineeringWriting clear instructions and examples so the model does what you mean.Saaf instructions aur examples likhna taaki model wahi kare jo aap chahte ho.Ch 6
Context windowThe most tokens a model can read in one call: instructions, history, documents and its own answer together.Ek call mein model jitne tokens padh sakta hai: instructions, history, documents aur uska jawab milakar.Ch 2

Context compactionSummarising old parts of a long conversation so it fits, keeping decisions and dropping chatter.Lambi baatcheet ke purane hisson ka saar banana taaki fit ho, faisle rakho, faaltu baatein hatao.Ch 10

GraphRAGRAG over a knowledge graph: follow relationships between entities to answer questions that span many documents.Knowledge graph par RAG: entities ke rishte follow karke aise sawaal suljhao jo kai documents mein faile hon.Ch 14
EmbeddingsLists of numbers that capture meaning; similar texts get nearby numbers.Numbers ki list jo matlab pakadti hai; milte-julte text ke numbers paas-paas.Ch 13
Vector searchFinding the stored embeddings closest to your question's embedding.Sawaal ke embedding ke sabse paas rakhe embeddings dhoondhna.Ch 13
Semantic searchSearch by meaning, not exact words: "leave policy" also finds "holiday rules".Matlab se search, hu-ba-hu shabdon se nahi: "leave policy" se "chhutti ke niyam" bhi mil jaata hai.Ch 13

Prompt cachingReusing a repeated prompt prefix so later calls are cheaper and faster.Baar-baar aane wale prompt ke shuruaati hisse ko dobara use karna taaki agli calls sasti aur tez hon.Ch 29

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Prompt engineering improves the words; context engineering manages everything in the window; harness engineering designs the whole system around the model.
  2. Most "new" terms are a known idea with a new name: look it up here, then read the chapter it points to.
  3. Some acronyms (MLM, RLM) have more than one meaning: check the context.
  1. Prompt engineering shabd sudhaarti hai; context engineering window ki har cheez sambhaalti hai; harness engineering model ke aas-paas poora system design karti hai.
  2. Zyaadatar "naye" shabd naye naam wala purana idea hain: yahan dekho, phir bataaya chapter padho.
  3. Kuch acronyms (MLM, RLM) ke ek se zyaada matlab hain: context dekho.
