Advanced PromptingAdvanced Prompting

Clear briefs get you most of the way. For the rest, prompt engineers use a handful of techniques with fancy names. Each one is a simple idea a good teacher already uses: show examples, ask the student to work step by step, split a big job into small ones, ask for a second check, and take a vote when unsure.

Saaf briefing se zyaadatar kaam ho jaata hai. Baaki ke liye prompt engineers kuch techniques use karte hain jinke naam bhaari hain. Har ek ek seedha idea hai jo achha teacher pehle se use karta hai: examples dikhao, step by step kaam karwao, bade kaam ko chhote kaamon mein baanto, doosra check maango, aur shak ho toh vote lo.

1

Show, don't tell (few-shot)Batao nahi, dikhao (few-shot)

Some things are easier to show than to describe: a tone, a label set, an exact format. Put two to five examples of input → ideal output in the prompt, and the model copies the pattern. Vary the examples so it learns the pattern, not one surface detail.

Kuch cheezein samjhaane se zyaada dikhaana aasaan hai: tone, labels ka set, sateek format. Prompt mein input → behtareen output ke do se paanch examples rakho, aur model pattern copy karta hai. Examples alag-alag rakho taaki woh pattern seekhe, ek upari detail nahi.

2

Think first, then answerPehle socho, phir jawab do

Haron writes one token at a time, and it can't take back a token. If it blurts the answer first, the answer is a guess. If it writes out the steps first, each step becomes part of its context, and the final answer is built on them. This is chain-of-thought prompting. Today's reasoning models (Chapter 9) do this internally, so for them you mostly just ask the question clearly.

Haron ek baar mein ek token likhta hai, aur token wapas nahi le sakta. Agar woh jawab pehle bol de, toh jawab andaaza hai. Agar pehle steps likhe, toh har step uske context ka hissa ban jaata hai, aur aakhri jawab un par bana hota hai. Ise chain-of-thought prompting kehte hain. Aaj ke reasoning models (Chapter 9) yeh andar hi karte hain, isliye unke liye aksar bas sawaal saaf poochna kaafi hai.

3

An assembly line of prompts (chaining)Prompts ki assembly line (chaining)

One giant prompt that does everything is hard to debug. Split the job into steps, each with its own small prompt, and pass the output of one as the input to the next. Every step can be tested, cached, retried, and even given to a cheaper model.

Sab kuch karne wala ek vishaal prompt debug karna mushkil hai. Kaam ko steps mein baanto, har ek ka apna chhota prompt, aur ek ka output agle ka input banao. Har step test, cache, retry ho sakta hai, aur sasta model bhi use kar sakta hai.

4

When unsure, take a vote (self-consistency)Shak ho toh vote lo (self-consistency)

Ask the same question five times with some randomness, and pick the most common answer. Mistakes tend to scatter; correct reasoning tends to agree. It costs five times as much, so use it for important, checkable answers like maths, classification and extraction.

Same sawaal thodi randomness ke saath paanch baar poochho, aur sabse aam jawab chuno. Galtiyaan bikhar jaati hain; sahi reasoning aksar ek jaisi hoti hai. Paanch guna kharch hai, isliye zaroori, check hone laayak jawabon ke liye use karo jaise maths, classification aur extraction.

Engineer's note

Let the model write the first draft of your promptPrompt ka pehla draft model se likhwao

Meta-prompting: describe your task, users and failure cases to a strong model and ask it to write a system prompt, including examples. Then test that prompt on your eval set and edit by hand. Provider consoles have prompt generators and improvers built in. It's a draft, not a finished product.

Meta-prompting: majboot model ko apna kaam, users aur failure cases batao aur use system prompt likhne ko kaho, examples samet. Phir us prompt ko apne eval set par test karo aur haath se edit karo. Provider consoles mein prompt generators aur improvers bane hote hain. Yeh draft hai, taiyaar product nahi.

AI behaviour

Examples are copied very faithfullyExamples bahut wafaadaari se copy hote hain

If all your examples are about laptops, Haron may assume every ticket is about laptops. If all are short, every answer becomes short. Models copy length, tone, vocabulary and even mistakes in examples. Vary them on purpose, and check that they don't contain anything you don't want repeated.

Agar aapke saare examples laptops ke baare mein hain, Haron maan sakta hai ki har ticket laptop ka hai. Agar sab chhote hain, har jawab chhota ho jaata hai. Models examples ki lambaai, tone, shabd aur galtiyaan tak copy karte hain. Jaan-boojh kar unhe alag rakho, aur check karo ki unme kuch aisa na ho jo aap dohraana nahi chahte.

In the real world

Old tricks that stopped matteringPurane tricks jo ab kaam ke nahi

"I'll tip you $200", "my job depends on this", "take a deep breath": these had small effects on old models and are mostly noise now. Assistant-message prefill (starting the model's reply for it) is no longer accepted by current Claude models; use structured outputs instead (Chapter 8). What lasts: clarity, context, examples, decomposition and evaluation.

"Main $200 tip doonga", "meri naukri iss par tiki hai", "lambi saans lo": purane models par inka chhota asar tha aur ab zyaadatar shor hai. Assistant-message prefill (model ka jawab uske liye shuru karna) ab current Claude models accept nahi karte; iske badle structured outputs use karo (Chapter 8). Jo tikta hai: saaf baat, context, examples, kaam ko todna aur evaluation.

✓

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Show me 2–5 varied examples when the pattern is hard to describe.
  2. Let me write my steps before my answer, unless I'm a reasoning model that does it inside.
  3. Chain small prompts instead of one giant one.
  4. Vote across several answers when it matters; let me draft your prompts, then test them.
  1. Jab pattern samjhaana mushkil ho, mujhe 2–5 alag examples dikhao.
  2. Jawab se pehle mujhe steps likhne do, jab tak main andar sochne wala reasoning model na hoon.
  3. Ek vishaal prompt ki jagah chhote prompts ki chain banao.
  4. Zaroori ho toh kai jawabon par vote lo; apne prompts ka draft mujhse likhwao, phir test karo.
