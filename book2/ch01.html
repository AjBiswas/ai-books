What an AI Engineer DoesAI Engineer Kya Karta Hai

You've used ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Maybe you've even pasted their answers into your work. Now you want to build with them: a support bot for your company, an app that reads invoices, an agent that books meetings. The person who does that is an AI engineer. Your mentor for this book is Haron, the robot you may know from Book 1. Haron knows how models think; you'll learn how to make them useful.

Aapne ChatGPT, Claude ya Gemini use kiya hai. Shayad unke jawab apne kaam mein paste bhi kiye hain. Ab aap unke saath kuch banana chahte ho: company ke liye support bot, invoices padhne wali app, meetings book karne wala agent. Yeh kaam karne wala insaan hai AI engineer. Is book mein aapka mentor Haron hai, woh robot jise aap Book 1 se jaante honge. Haron jaanta hai models kaise sochte hain; aap seekhoge unhe kaam ka kaise banaana hai.

1

Three jobs that sound alikeTeen kaam jo milte-julte lagte hain

Think of cars. One person designs engines, one builds the factory that makes them, and one builds a taxi service that uses the cars. All three need to understand cars, but their days look very different. AI works the same way.

Gaadiyon ka socho. Ek insaan engine design karta hai, ek unhe banaane wali factory banata hai, aur ek gaadiyon se taxi service banata hai. Teeno ko gaadi samajhni padti hai, par unke din bilkul alag dikhte hain. AI mein bhi aisa hi hai.

RememberYaad rakho

An AI engineer mostly doesn't train models. You take a model someone else trained, and build everything around it: the instructions, the knowledge it reads, the tools it uses, the checks, the cost controls and the user experience. Good Python and good judgement matter more than advanced maths.

AI engineer zyaadatar models train nahi karta. Aap kisi aur ka train kiya model lete ho, aur uske aas-paas sab kuch banaate ho: instructions, jo gyaan woh padhe, jo tools woh use kare, checks, kharch ka control aur user experience. Advanced maths se zyaada achha Python aur achhi samajh kaam aati hai.

Engineer's note

The loop never ends, and that's the jobYeh loop kabhi khatam nahi hota, aur yahi kaam hai

The last box points back to the start. Real users ask things you never imagined, models get updated, costs change. An AI engineer's week is mostly reading failures, adding them to the test set, and improving the prompt, the data or the tools, then proving the change helped.

Aakhri box wapas shuruaat ki taraf jaata hai. Asli users aisi baatein poochte hain jo aapne socha bhi nahi, models update hote hain, kharch badalta hai. AI engineer ka hafta zyaadatar failures padhne, unhe test set mein jodne, aur prompt, data ya tools sudhaarne mein jaata hai, phir saabit karne mein ki badlaav ne madad ki.

3

Your skills mapAapka skills naksha

AI behaviour

Demos are easy; reliability is the jobDemo aasaan hai; bharosemandi asli kaam hai

A model can do something impressive in a five-minute demo and fail one time in ten in real use. It may make up facts, change its answer when pushed, or go off track on long tasks. Most of this book is about the gap between "it worked once" and "it works for 10,000 customers".

Model paanch minute ke demo mein kamaal kar sakta hai aur asli use mein das mein se ek baar fail ho sakta hai. Woh facts bana sakta hai, dabaav par jawab badal sakta hai, ya lambe kaam mein bhatak sakta hai. Yeh book zyaadatar "ek baar chal gaya" aur "10,000 customers ke liye chalta hai" ke beech ke farak ke baare mein hai.

In the real world

Job titles vary; the work doesn'tJob titles alag, kaam wahi

You'll see "AI engineer", "LLM engineer", "GenAI developer", "applied AI engineer" and sometimes "prompt engineer". The interviews test the same things: can you build a working RAG or agent app, explain why it fails, measure it, and keep it cheap and safe. Chapter 34 turns this book into a portfolio that proves it.

Aapko "AI engineer", "LLM engineer", "GenAI developer", "applied AI engineer" aur kabhi "prompt engineer" dikhega. Interviews same cheezein test karte hain: kya aap chalne wali RAG ya agent app bana sakte ho, samjha sakte ho ki woh kyun fail hoti hai, use naap sakte ho, aur sasti aur surakshit rakh sakte ho. Chapter 34 is book ko aisa portfolio banata hai jo yeh saabit kare.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. An AI engineer builds products with models; others build the models.
  2. Every AI feature goes around a loop: prompt, knowledge, tools, test, ship, watch, improve.
  3. The skills stack from basics up to production and product sense.
  4. You can start today with a laptop and no money.
