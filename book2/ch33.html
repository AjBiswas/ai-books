Judge the next buzzword yourselfAgla buzzword khud parkho

AI behaviour

Demos are chosen; your data isn'tDemos chune hue hote hain; aapka data nahi

Launch videos show the best run out of many. Benchmarks are often saturated or partly in training data. Before adopting anything new, run it on 20 of your real cases with your eval. Half of all "game-changers" turn out to be a small improvement, and that's still worth knowing.

Launch videos kai runs mein se sabse achha dikhaate hain. Benchmarks aksar saturate ya aanshik roop se training data mein hote hain. Kuch naya apnaane se pehle use apne 20 asli cases par apne eval se chalao. Aadhe "game-changers" chhota sudhaar nikalte hain, aur woh jaanna bhi keemti hai.

Engineer's note

How to stay current without drowningDoobe bina taaza kaise rahein

Follow a few primary sources (provider engineering blogs and docs, a couple of practitioner newsletters or podcasts, arXiv for papers you need), spend one hour a week trying one new thing on your laptop, and ignore the rest. The fundamentals in this book change slowly; the product names change monthly.

Kuch mool sources follow karo (provider engineering blogs aur docs, ek-do practitioner newsletters ya podcasts, zaroori papers ke liye arXiv), hafte mein ek ghanta laptop par ek nayi cheez try karo, aur baaki ignore. Is book ki buniyaadi baatein dheere badalti hain; product ke naam har mahine.
