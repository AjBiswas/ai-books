Budgets for EveryoneSabke liye Budget

At Acme Global, the monthly AI bill arrives as one big number. The CFO asks three questions: Who spent it? Was it worth it? Can we stop a runaway agent before it spends next month's money tonight? Answering them is called FinOps for AI, and it is built from three simple things: nested wallets, a rate limiter, and a ledger.

Acme Global mein mahine ka AI bill ek bade number ki tarah aata hai. CFO teen sawaal poochte hain: Kisne kharch kiya? Kya yeh faayde ka tha? Kya hum bhaagte hue agent ko aaj raat agle mahine ka paisa kharch karne se pehle rok sakte hain? In sawaalon ka jawab dena AI ke liye FinOps kehlaata hai, aur yeh teen seedhi cheezon se banta hai: nested wallets, rate limiter, aur ledger.

1

Wallets inside walletsWallets ke andar wallets

Every scope has a monthly wallet: the company, each client project, each team, and even a single bot. A request is charged to every wallet above it, and it is stopped if any of them is empty. Here is one simulated month from this chapter's lab.

Har scope ka mahine ka wallet hai: company, har client project, har team, aur ek akela bot bhi. Request apne upar ke har wallet se charge hoti hai, aur koi bhi khaali ho toh ruk jaati hai. Yeh is chapter ke lab ka ek simulated mahina hai.

RememberYaad rakho

A soft limit (80%) sends an alert and can switch the scope to a cheaper model tier. A hard limit (100%) blocks new requests with a clear message and a way to ask for more. Never let a budget silently cut an agent off in the middle of a write action; check the budget before each step.

Soft limit (80%) alert bhejta hai aur scope ko saste model tier par daal sakta hai. Hard limit (100%) nayi requests saaf message aur aur maangne ke raaste ke saath rokta hai. Budget ko kabhi bhi agent ko write action ke beech chupchaap rokne mat do; har step se pehle budget check karo.

2

The coin bucket (rate limits)Sikkon ki baalti (rate limits)

Budgets protect the month; rate limits protect the minute. A buggy script can send 10,000 requests in a minute and hit the provider's limits for the whole company. Each tenant gets a bucket of coins: every request takes a coin, and coins drip back in at a fixed rate. A full bucket allows a short burst; an empty one says "try again in a moment" (HTTP 429).

Budget mahine ki raksha karta hai; rate limit minute ki. Ek bugwala script minute mein 10,000 requests bhej kar poori company ke liye provider ki limits khatam kar sakta hai. Har tenant ko sikkon ki baalti milti hai: har request ek sikka leti hai, aur sikke ek tay raftaar se wapas tapakte hain. Bhari baalti chhota burst allow karti hai; khaali baalti kehti hai "thodi der mein try karo" (HTTP 429).

Where the savings come fromBachat kahan se aati hai

An illustrative example: a team's ₹8,00,000 monthly bill, and the levers applied one after another, cheapest first. Your numbers will differ; measure each lever on your own traffic and check that quality holds.

Ek example: ek team ka ₹8,00,000 ka mahine ka bill, aur ek ke baad ek levers, sabse saste pehle. Aapke numbers alag honge; har lever apne traffic par naapo aur check karo ki quality bani rahe.

AI behaviour

Haron doesn't know the budget unless you tell itHaron ko budget tab tak nahi pata jab tak aap na batao

A model has no sense of cost. It will read a 400-page PDF to answer a yes/no question. Tell it the budget in the prompt or with the provider's task-budget feature, give it cheap tools first (search before read), and stop it from outside when the limit is reached.

Model ko kharch ka koi ehsaas nahi. Haan/na ke sawaal ke liye bhi 400 page ka PDF padh lega. Prompt mein ya provider ke task-budget feature se use budget batao, pehle saste tools do (padhne se pehle search), aur limit aane par bahar se roko.

Enterprise

Chargeback turns AI from a cost into a productChargeback AI ko kharch se product banata hai

When each client project and team sees its own AI line on the monthly report, three good things happen: teams optimise their own usage, finance can price AI into client contracts, and leaders can compare cost with value per use case. Report cost per successful task next to cost, so cheap-but-useless bots don't look like winners.

Jab har client project aur team mahine ki report mein apni AI line dekhti hai, teen achhi cheezein hoti hain: teams apna use khud optimise karti hain, finance AI ko client contracts mein jod sakta hai, aur leaders har use case ka kharch aur faayda compare kar sakte hain. Kharch ke saath har safal kaam ka kharch bhi dikhaao, taaki saste-par-bekaar bots jeetne wale na lagein.

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Every scope has a wallet; I'm charged to all the wallets above me.
  2. At 80% the team warns and may switch me to a cheaper tier; at 100% it stops me cleanly.
  3. A coin bucket stops floods of requests.
  4. Caching, routing, short answers and batching are the big savings. Measure cost per successful task.
  1. Har scope ka wallet hai; mera kharch mere upar ke saare wallets se katta hai.
  2. 80% par team chetaavni deti hai aur mujhe saste tier par daal sakti hai; 100% par saaf tareeke se rokti hai.
  3. Sikkon ki baalti requests ki baadh rokti hai.
  4. Caching, routing, chhote jawab aur batching badi bachat hain. Har safal kaam ka kharch naapo.
