Chapter 11 · Haron goes to a big companyChapter 11 · Haron badi company mein

The Company BlueprintCompany ka Naksha

Haron has a new employer: Acme Global, an IT services company with 3,00,000 employees and hundreds of clients, among them a bank in Mumbai, a retailer in Germany and its own HR department. Each client has different rules: which models are allowed, where data may travel, how much may be spent. If every team builds its own harness, nobody can see the total spend and nobody can prove the rules are followed. So Acme builds one front door for all AI traffic: the AI gateway.

Haron ko naya employer mila: Acme Global, ek IT services company jisme 3,00,000 employees aur saikadon clients hain, jinme Mumbai ka ek bank, Germany ka ek retailer aur company ka apna HR department hai. Har client ke alag niyam hain: kaunse models allowed hain, data kahan ja sakta hai, kitna kharch ho sakta hai. Agar har team apna harness banaaye, toh na koi kul kharch dekh paayega na koi saabit kar paayega ki niyam maane gaye. Isliye Acme saare AI traffic ke liye ek main darwaaza banati hai: AI gateway.

The right model for the jobKaam ke liye sahi model

Sending every request to the biggest model is like sending every parcel by air: fast and great, but ruinously expensive. The router looks at each request, estimates how hard it is, and picks the cheapest tier that the tenant allows and that can do the job.

Har request sabse bade model ko bhejna har parcel hawai jahaz se bhejne jaisa hai: tez aur badhiya, par bahut mehenga. Router har request dekhta hai, andaaza lagata hai kitni mushkil hai, aur sabse sasta tier chunta hai jo tenant allow kare aur kaam kar sake.

AI behaviour

Routing changes behaviour, not just costRouting behaviour bhi badalta hai, sirf kharch nahi

A smaller model may follow your format less reliably, refuse more, or call tools differently. A fallback from one provider to another mid-conversation can change tone and even answers. So every route (tenant × task × model) needs its own eval results (Chapter 16), and fallbacks should go to a model that has passed the same evals.

Chhota model aapka format kam bharose se follow kar sakta hai, zyaada mana kar sakta hai, ya tools alag tarah call kar sakta hai. Baatcheet ke beech ek provider se doosre par fallback tone aur jawab bhi badal sakta hai. Isliye har route (tenant × kaam × model) ke apne eval results chahiye (Chapter 16), aur fallback usi model par jaaye jo same evals pass kar chuka ho.

Enterprise

Build or buy the gateway?Gateway banaayein ya khareedein?

You don't have to write it all. Open-source proxies (for example LiteLLM), commercial AI gateways (Portkey, Kong AI Gateway and others) and cloud platforms (Amazon Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry, Google Vertex AI) cover routing, keys, rate limits and logging. Companies usually buy the plumbing and build the policy: their own tenant rules, PII handling for local laws, chargeback and evals. Whatever you choose, keep the interface your teams code against stable, so the product underneath can change.

Sab kuch khud likhna zaroori nahi. Open-source proxies (jaise LiteLLM), commercial AI gateways (Portkey, Kong AI Gateway aur doosre) aur cloud platforms (Amazon Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry, Google Vertex AI) routing, keys, rate limits aur logging sambhaal lete hain. Companies aksar plumbing khareedti hain aur policy khud banaati hain: apne tenant niyam, local kanoon ke hisaab se PII handling, chargeback aur evals. Jo bhi chuno, teams jis interface par code karti hain use sthir rakho, taaki neeche ka product badal sake.

For coders: three tenants through one gatewayCoders ke liye: ek gateway se teen tenantscode/harness/gateway.py 
gw = Gateway(
    providers={"cloud": cloud_provider, "onprem": vllm_provider},
    model_provider={"mock-small": "cloud", "mock-medium": "cloud", "mock-large": "cloud",
                    "vllm/onprem-70b": "onprem"},
    policies={
        "retail": TenantPolicy("retail", region="eu", allowed_models={"mock-small", "mock-medium", "mock-large"}),
        "bank":   TenantPolicy("bank", region="in", allowed_models={"vllm/onprem-70b"}, pii_mode="block"),
        "hr":     TenantPolicy("hr", region="in", allowed_models={"mock-small", "mock-medium"}),
    },
    router=Router([Tier("mock-small", 0.35), Tier("mock-medium", 0.65), Tier("mock-large", 1.0),
                   Tier("vllm/onprem-70b", 1.0)], fallbacks={"mock-small": ["mock-medium"], ...}),
)

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. In a big company, all AI traffic goes through one front door.
  2. The door checks identity, policy, budget and rate, then routes, falls back if needed, meters and records.
  3. Pick the cheapest model that can do the job and that the client allows.
  4. Buy the plumbing; build your own rules.
  1. Badi company mein saara AI traffic ek main darwaaze se jaata hai.
  2. Darwaaza pehchaan, policy, budget aur rate check karta hai, phir route karta hai, zaroorat ho toh fallback, naapta hai aur record karta hai.
  3. Sabse sasta model chuno jo kaam kar sake aur client allow kare.
  4. Plumbing khareedo; apne niyam khud banao.
