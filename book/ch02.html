Haron's WalletHaron ka Wallet

On its first day at work, Haron gets a wallet. Every word it reads costs a tiny coin. Every word it writes costs a bigger coin. And because Haron forgets everything, the team must hand over the whole diary again on every turn, so the coins add up much faster than anyone expects.

Kaam ke pehle din Haron ko ek wallet milta hai. Har word jo woh padhta hai, ek chhota sikka lagta hai. Har word jo woh likhta hai, ek bada sikka. Aur kyunki Haron sab bhool jaata hai, team ko har turn par poori diary dobara deni padti hai, isliye sikke kisi ki soch se kahin zyaada tezi se jud jaate hain.

1

The bill that keeps growingBadhta hua bill

A customer asks Haron five short questions. Each question is tiny, about 200 tokens. But on every turn, the team sends Haron the rules (2,000 tokens) plus everything said so far. Watch what Haron actually reads.

Ek customer Haron se paanch chhote sawaal poochta hai. Har sawaal chhota hai, lagbhag 200 tokens. Lekin har turn par team Haron ko niyam (2,000 tokens) aur ab tak ki poori baatcheet bhejti hai. Dekho Haron asal mein kitna padhta hai.

RememberYaad rakho

In a chat or an agent loop, total input grows roughly with the square of the number of turns. Turn 40 pays for turns 1 to 39 again. That's why long agent runs get expensive, and why caching and compaction exist.

Chat ya agent loop mein total input turns ki sankhya ke lagbhag square ke hisaab se badhta hai. Turn 40 par turn 1 se 39 ka paisa phir lagta hai. Isliye lambe agent runs mehenge padte hain, aur isiliye caching aur compaction bane hain.

2

The diary has a size limitDiary ki ek limit hai

Haron's diary can hold only so many tokens: this is the context window. Today's big models hold 200,000 to 1,000,000 tokens, which is several thick books. But a bigger diary has two catches: every page costs money each turn, and Haron reads the far pages less carefully.

Haron ki diary mein sirf itne hi tokens aa sakte hain: ise context window kehte hain. Aaj ke bade models 2,00,000 se 10,00,000 tokens tak rakh sakte hain, yaani kai moti kitaabein. Lekin badi diary ki do dikkatein hain: har page har turn par paisa leta hai, aur door ke pages Haron kam dhyaan se padhta hai.

AI behaviour

Big window, smaller "useful" windowBadi window, chhoti "kaam ki" window

A model that accepts 1M tokens doesn't use all of them equally well. As the diary fills, accuracy on finding and combining facts usually drops; researchers call this context rot. Tests like "needle in a haystack" are easy; tasks that need many facts from many places are much harder. So a harness keeps the diary as small as the task allows, even when the window is huge.

Jo model 10 lakh tokens le sakta hai, woh sabko barabar achhe se use nahi karta. Diary bharne ke saath facts dhoondhne aur jodne ki accuracy aksar girti hai; researchers ise context rot kehte hain. "Bhoose mein sui" jaise test aasaan hain; kai jagah se kai facts jodne wale kaam bahut mushkil. Isliye harness diary ko kaam ki zaroorat jitna chhota rakhta hai, chahe window kitni bhi badi ho.

Harness note

Measure cost per finished task, not per requestHar request nahi, har poore kaam ka kharch naapo

A cheap model that needs 9 tries and a human fix can cost more than an expensive model that gets it right once. The harness should log, for every task: model calls, input/output/cached tokens, tool calls, retries, and whether the task succeeded. Then compare cost ÷ successful tasks.

Sasta model jise 9 baar try karna pade aur phir insaan theek kare, woh ek baar mein sahi karne wale mehenge model se zyaada mehenga pad sakta hai. Harness har kaam ke liye log kare: model calls, input/output/cached tokens, tool calls, retries, aur kaam safal hua ya nahi. Phir kharch ÷ safal kaam compare karo.

Enterprise

Prices change; models retireDaam badalte hain; models retire hote hain

Provider prices change several times a year, and models are deprecated on a schedule. Keep prices in config, not code (this book's harness/pricing.py is one dictionary), record the price used with every audit record, and re-run your cost model when a new tier appears. A new model at the same price can use a different tokenizer and change your bill by 30%.

Providers ke daam saal mein kai baar badalte hain, aur models tay schedule par band hote hain. Daam code mein nahi, config mein rakho (is book ki harness/pricing.py bas ek dictionary hai), har audit record ke saath use hua daam save karo, aur naya tier aaye toh cost model dobara chalao. Same daam wala naya model alag tokenizer use karke bill 30% badal sakta hai.

Levers, roughly in order of "free first": (1) prompt caching, (2) trimming what you send (tool results, old turns), (3) output caps and terse formats, (4) batch processing for non-urgent work (about 50% off on major providers), (5) lower effort where quality holds, (6) routing easy work to a smaller tier.

✓

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. Reading costs a little, writing costs more, and thinking counts as writing.
  2. Every turn re-sends the whole diary, so cost grows fast with turns.
  3. A big window isn't a perfect memory. Keep the diary small.
  4. Caching makes the repeated part about 90% cheaper.
  5. Measure cost per successful task, per language, per model.
  1. Padhna sasta, likhna mehenga, aur sochna bhi likhne mein ginta hai.
  2. Har turn poori diary dobara bhejta hai, isliye turns ke saath kharch tezi se badhta hai.
  3. Badi window perfect yaaddasht nahi. Diary chhoti rakho.
  4. Caching dohraaye hisse ko lagbhag 90% sasta kar deti hai.
  5. Kharch har safal kaam, har bhasha, har model ke hisaab se naapo.
