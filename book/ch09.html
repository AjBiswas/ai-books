Many HaronsKai Saare Haron

Some jobs are too big for one diary. "Research our three competitors" means reading dozens of pages about each. If one Haron reads everything, its diary overflows and it forgets the first competitor by the time it reaches the third. So a lead Haron splits the job and sends workers, each with a fresh, empty diary. This is powerful, and expensive, and easy to overuse.

Kuch kaam ek diary ke liye bahut bade hain. "Hamare teen competitors par research karo" matlab har ek ke baare mein darjanon pages padhna. Agar ek hi Haron sab padhe, toh diary overflow ho jaayegi aur teesre tak pahunchte-pahunchte pehla bhool jaayega. Isliye ek lead Haron kaam baant kar workers bhejta hai, har ek ki nayi, khaali diary ke saath. Yeh taakatwar hai, mehenga hai, aur iska zaroorat se zyaada use aasaan hai.

The price of a teamTeam ki keemat

Every worker re-reads its own instructions, does its own searching and writes its own report, and the lead reads all the reports. Anthropic reported that in their research system, single agents used about 4× the tokens of a chat, and multi-agent runs about 15×. Details can also get lost: each summary is a chance to drop the one fact that mattered, like a game of telephone.

Har worker apne instructions dobara padhta hai, apni search karta hai, apni report likhta hai, aur lead saari reports padhta hai. Anthropic ne bataaya ki unke research system mein ek agent chat se lagbhag 4× tokens use karta tha, aur multi-agent runs lagbhag 15×. Details bhi kho sakti hain: har summary ek mauka hai woh ek fact girane ka jo zaroori tha, "chinese whispers" khel jaisa.

3

Passing the customer on (handoff)Customer ko aage bhejna (handoff)

A different shape: specialists in a line. A front-desk Haron sorts the request and hands the customer to the billing Haron, which has billing tools and billing rules. The key is the handoff note: what the next agent must know, so the customer never repeats themselves.

Ek alag shape: line mein specialists. Front-desk Haron request ko chhaant kar customer ko billing Haron ke paas bhejta hai, jiske paas billing tools aur billing niyam hain. Asli cheez hai handoff note: agle agent ko kya jaanna zaroori hai, taaki customer ko apni baat dobara na kehni pade.

AI behaviour

Workers overdo, duplicate and driftWorkers zyaada karte hain, dohraate hain, aur bhatakte hain

Given a vague subtask ("research pricing"), workers often search the same sources as each other, go far beyond what was needed, or answer a slightly different question. The fix is in the lead's instructions: each subtask needs a goal, a boundary ("only Q3 2026, only India"), an output format, and an effort hint ("5 searches at most").

Dhundhla subtask ("pricing research karo") milne par workers aksar ek-doosre jaise hi sources search karte hain, zaroorat se bahut aage chale jaate hain, ya thoda alag sawaal ka jawab dete hain. Ilaaj lead ke instructions mein hai: har subtask mein lakshya, seema ("sirf Q3 2026, sirf India"), output format, aur mehnat ka ishaara ("zyaada se zyaada 5 searches") ho.

Enterprise

Budget and permissions per workerHar worker ka budget aur permissions

A team of agents multiplies spend and blast radius. Give each worker its own token budget and its own tool permissions (a research worker needs read-only web access; it never needs the refund tool). Use a cheaper model for workers and the stronger model for the lead. Trace the whole tree as one run so finance can see the total (Chapter 17).

Agents ki team kharch aur nuksaan ka daayra dono badhaati hai. Har worker ko apna token budget aur apni tool permissions do (research worker ko sirf read-only web chahiye; refund tool kabhi nahi). Workers ke liye sasta model aur lead ke liye majboot model use karo. Poore tree ko ek run ki tarah trace karo taaki finance kul kharch dekh sake (Chapter 17).

What we learnedHumne kya seekha

  1. A lead can split big, independent work across workers with fresh diaries.
  2. Teams cost many times more tokens and can lose details in summaries.
  3. Handoffs need a good note so nobody repeats themselves.
  4. Use one agent unless the job truly splits.
  1. Lead bade, alag-alag kaam ko nayi diaries wale workers mein baant sakta hai.
  2. Teams kai guna zyaada tokens leti hain aur summaries mein details kho sakti hain.
  3. Handoff ke liye achha note chahiye taaki kisi ko baat dohraani na pade.
  4. Jab tak kaam sach mein na bate, ek agent use karo.
