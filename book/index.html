Building the Harness

Haron is a robot that can only guess the next word. Follow its story to learn how AI models really behave, how to build the team around them, and how a big company runs that team with its own rules and budgets. Every idea comes with a picture, an animation and a simple example.

Haron ek robot hai jo sirf agla word guess kar sakta hai. Uski kahani ke saath seekho ki AI models asal mein kaise behave karte hain, unke aas-paas ki team kaise banti hai, aur ek badi company apne niyam aur budget ke saath woh team kaise chalaati hai. Har idea ke saath picture, animation aur aasaan example hai.
