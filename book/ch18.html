The Company HarnessCompany ka Harness

Haron started this book as a brain that could only guess the next word. Now it works at Acme Global with a full team around it: a gateway that knows every client's rules, a loop that knows when to stop, tools behind a strict guard, a diary packed with care, hooks that can't be argued with, budgets, security layers, exams and dashboards. In this chapter we switch it all on together, follow real customers through it, ship a change safely, and plan how a real company rolls this out.

Is book ki shuruaat mein Haron ek dimaag tha jo sirf agla word guess kar sakta tha. Ab woh Acme Global mein poori team ke saath kaam karta hai: har client ke niyam jaanne wala gateway, kab rukna hai jaanne wala loop, sakht guard ke peeche tools, dhyaan se pack ki gayi diary, baaton se na maanne wale hooks, budgets, security layers, exams aur dashboards. Is chapter mein hum sab ek saath chaalu karenge, asli customers ko usme se guzarte dekhenge, ek badlaav surakshit ship karenge, aur plan karenge ki asli company ise kaise laagu karti hai.

AI behaviour

The model will keep changing; the harness is what staysModel badalta rahega; harness tika rehta hai

Every few months a new model arrives: smarter, cheaper, with new quirks. A company that built its value into the harness (policies, tools, context, evals, data) swaps the brain in days and measures the difference. A company that built its value into prompt tricks for one model starts again each time.

Har kuch mahine mein naya model aata hai: zyaada samajhdaar, sasta, naye nakhron ke saath. Jis company ne apni value harness mein banaayi (policies, tools, context, evals, data), woh dimaag dinon mein badal leti hai aur farak naap leti hai. Jisne ek model ke prompt tricks mein value banaayi, woh har baar shuru se shuru karti hai.

The team that runs itIse chalaane wali team

A typical AI platform team: platform engineers (gateway, adapters, infra), an evals and quality lead, a security engineer focused on AI threats, a FinOps analyst, a governance lead working with legal, and developer-advocates who help product teams build agents. Product teams own their agents, prompts, tools and eval cases; the platform team owns the rails.

Aam AI platform team: platform engineers (gateway, adapters, infra), evals aur quality lead, AI khatron par focused security engineer, FinOps analyst, legal ke saath kaam karne wala governance lead, aur developer-advocates jo product teams ko agents banaane mein madad karte hain. Product teams apne agents, prompts, tools aur eval cases ki maalik hain; platform team patriyon (rails) ki.
